cluster — feed contrast mockup

Three alternative color balances for the feed header and bottom nav. The void (#1A0A10) and card surface (#2E1420 Sangiovese) are unchanged — only the nav shell colors shift. Goal: make the header and tab bar read as distinct layers from the card, without breaking the warm burgundy palette established in the Cluster brand guide.

Variant A

Barolo shelf

Header + nav use the brand's deepest red, Barolo #4A0E1F. The nav sits darker than the card, inverting the usual surface hierarchy so the nav reads as a sunken shelf at the top and bottom of the screen. Feels anchored, editorial, closest to the current brand icon treatment.

card   #2E1420 Sangiovese
void   #1A0A10

Variant B

Nebbiolo shelf

Same approximate lightness as today's Malbec nav, but shifts the hue toward Nebbiolo (the brand's purple-wine tone, #4A3060). Contrast comes from the hue difference rather than lightness — nav feels subtly "other" without dominating. Best bet if you want separation without making the chrome feel heavy.

Variant C

Glass over void

Nav becomes a translucent layer over the void with a blur — so the card material doesn't extend into the chrome. Strongest separation of the three and feels the most modern, but it's the biggest departure from the current "all warm surfaces" direction. Blur may not render on very old mobile GPUs.

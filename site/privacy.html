Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Overview

Cluster is a web and mobile application that allows users to log wine entries, upload photos, and connect with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

Cluster stores the account details and wine-log content needed to operate the application, including:

  • Profile information (username, display name)
  • Wine entries and tasting notes
  • Uploaded photos
  • Social connections
  • Feedback submissions

Operational logs and error telemetry may also be collected to maintain system reliability.

Privacy settings

Photos and entry metadata are access-controlled by your privacy settings (public, friends, or private). Signed URLs are used for photo delivery.

SMS authentication

If you provide your phone number to log into your account, Cluster may send a one-time verification code via SMS to authenticate your login.

  • Messages are transactional only
  • Messages are sent only when initiated by the user
  • Cluster does not send marketing or promotional messages

You may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages at any time. Reply HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies based on login activity.

AI processing

Certain features may process uploaded images or tasting notes through third-party AI services (such as OpenAI and Anthropic APIs) to provide autofill, recommendation, or summary assistance. Users should not upload sensitive personal content.

Data sharing

Cluster does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by trusted service providers necessary to operate the application, including:

  • Hosting infrastructure providers
  • Authentication services
  • AI processing services

These providers process data solely to provide application functionality.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are implemented to protect user information.

Data retention and deletion

Information is retained as long as necessary to operate the service and maintain account functionality. You can delete your account from within the app, which removes your profile, entries, and photos.

Age

Cluster is intended for people of legal drinking age in their country of residence.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact support@clusterwine.app.
